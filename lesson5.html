I am 25 years old.
मैं 25 साल का हूँ।
How old are you?
आपकी उम्र कितनी है?

📱 Phone Numbers

English mein phone number usually har digit ko separately bola ja sakta hai.

Example:

9876543210

Nine - Eight - Seven - Six - Five - Four - Three - Two - One - Zero
💡 Phone number practice karte waqt digits ko clearly aur slowly bolo.

🎭 Real Conversation

Person A: How many people are there in your family?
Person B: There are four people in my family.
Person A: How old are you?
Person B: I am twenty-five years old.
Person A: How much is this?
Person B: It costs one hundred rupees.

💡 Important Tips

1. 13–19 numbers ke end mein usually “teen” sound aati hai.

2. 20, 30, 40, 50 jaise numbers ko tens numbers kehte hain.

3. Age batane ke liye “I am … years old” use karo.

4. Price poochhne ke liye “How much is this?” bahut useful hai.

5. Phone number bolte waqt har digit clearly bolo.
