💡 English Speaking Tips

1️⃣ Don't be afraid of mistakes.
Mistakes are a normal part of learning.
2️⃣ Speak every day.
Even 5–10 minutes of speaking practice can help.
3️⃣ Think in English.
Try to make simple English sentences in your mind.
4️⃣ Listen carefully.
Listen to English conversations and repeat them.
5️⃣ Practice with SpeakMate AI.
Chat with AI and practice real conversations.
