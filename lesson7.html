💬 Real Shopping Conversation

Shopkeeper: Hello! Can I help you?

Customer: Yes, I am looking for a black shirt.

Shopkeeper: Sure. What size do you need?

Customer: I need a medium size.

Shopkeeper: Here you are.

Customer: How much is this?

Shopkeeper: It is 1,000 rupees.

Customer: Can you give me a discount?

Shopkeeper: I can give you 10% off.

Customer: Okay. I will take it.

💰 Polite Bargaining

Is there any discount?

❓ Shopping Questions & Answers

Q: How much is this?
A: It is five hundred rupees.
Q: Do you have a larger size?
A: Yes, we do.
Q: Do you have this in blue?
A: Yes, we have it in blue.
Q: Can I pay by card?
A: Yes, you can.
Q: Do you have a discount?
A: Yes, we can give you 10% off.

💡 English Speaking Tips

❌ Don't say: What price this?
✅ Say: How much is this?
❌ Don't say: Give discount.
✅ Say: Can you give me a discount?
❌ Don't say: I take this.
✅ Say: I will take it.
हमेशा polite English use करो। "Please", "Can you", और "Could you" conversation को ज्यादा natural बनाते हैं।
