👋 Lesson 1

Greetings & Polite English
Learn the most useful English greetings and polite expressions for everyday conversation.

🎯 What You Will Learn

In this lesson you will learn how to greet people, say good morning and good night, thank someone, introduce polite expressions and start simple conversations.

💡 Listen to each sentence and repeat it aloud. This will help improve your pronunciation.

👋 Basic Greetings

Hello
🇮🇳 नमस्ते / हेलो
🔊 Pronunciation: हेलो
💬 Example: Hello, how are you?
Hi
🇮🇳 नमस्ते / हाय
🔊 Pronunciation: हाय
💬 Example: Hi, nice to meet you.
Good Morning
🇮🇳 सुप्रभात
🔊 Pronunciation: गुड मॉर्निंग
💬 Example: Good morning, sir.
Good Afternoon
🇮🇳 शुभ दोपहर
🔊 Pronunciation: गुड आफ्टरनून
💬 Example: Good afternoon, everyone.
Good Evening
🇮🇳 शुभ संध्या
🔊 Pronunciation: गुड ईवनिंग
💬 Example: Good evening, welcome.
Good Night
🇮🇳 शुभ रात्रि
🔊 Pronunciation: गुड नाइट
💬 Example: Good night, see you tomorrow.

🙏 Polite Expressions

Thank You
🇮🇳 धन्यवाद
🔊 Pronunciation: थैंक यू
💬 Example: Thank you for your help.
You're Welcome
🇮🇳 आपका स्वागत है / कोई बात नहीं
🔊 Pronunciation: यू आर वेलकम
💬 Example: Thank you! — You're welcome.
Please
🇮🇳 कृपया
🔊 Pronunciation: प्लीज़
💬 Example: Please help me.
Sorry
🇮🇳 माफ़ कीजिए
🔊 Pronunciation: सॉरी
💬 Example: Sorry, I am late.
Excuse Me
🇮🇳 माफ़ कीजिए / सुनिए
🔊 Pronunciation: एक्सक्यूज़ मी
💬 Example: Excuse me, where is the station?
Nice to Meet You
🇮🇳 आपसे मिलकर खुशी हुई
🔊 Pronunciation: नाइस टू मीट यू
💬 Example: Nice to meet you, Manish.

💬 Useful Daily Sentences

How are you?
🇮🇳 आप कैसे हैं?
🔊 Pronunciation: हाउ आर यू?
💬 Example: Hello! How are you?
I am fine.
🇮🇳 मैं ठीक हूँ।
🔊 Pronunciation: आइ एम फाइन
💬 Example: I am fine, thank you.
What is your name?
🇮🇳 आपका नाम क्या है?
🔊 Pronunciation: व्हाट इज़ योर नेम?
💬 Example: Hi! What is your name?
My name is Manish.
🇮🇳 मेरा नाम मनीष है।
🔊 Pronunciation: माय नेम इज़ मनीष
💬 Example: Hello, my name is Manish.
See you later.
🇮🇳 बाद में मिलते हैं।
🔊 Pronunciation: सी यू लेटर
💬 Example: I have to go. See you later.
See you tomorrow.
🇮🇳 कल मिलते हैं।
🔊 Pronunciation: सी यू टुमॉरो
💬 Example: Good night. See you tomorrow.

🧠 Practice

Try speaking these sentences without looking at the Hindi meaning:

  1. Hello, how are you?
  2. Good morning!
  3. My name is Manish.
  4. Nice to meet you.
  5. Thank you very much.
  6. You're welcome.
  7. Excuse me.
  8. See you tomorrow.
